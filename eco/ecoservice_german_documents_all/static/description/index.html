German Documents - Professional Business Documents for Odoo

Enhance your business processes with perfectly formatted documents tailored for the German market! The "German Documents" module expands the document templates offered in Odoo. It adheres to common standards for documents used in Germany and provides essential templates for quotes, invoices, credit notes, and more.

Key Features:

  • Professional layouts for all business documents
  • Customizable fonts, headers, and footers
  • Multi-company support
  • Print on letterhead with logos and optional products
Ideal for businesses that need a consistent and professional appearance!
